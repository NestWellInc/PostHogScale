How to Find Duplicate Rows in a CSV

Duplicate rows are not always identical, and repeated values are not always errors. The safest first step is to define what makes a record unique and report suspected duplicates before deleting anything.

Exact duplicates versus business duplicates

An exact duplicate repeats every field in a row. A business duplicate repeats the fields that identify an entity, such as an order ID, while other fields may differ. The second case needs a rule for deciding which record is correct.

A safe duplicate-review workflow

  1. Preserve the source CSV.
  2. Identify the columns that should make each record unique.
  3. Group records with the same key.
  4. Compare conflicting fields inside each group.
  5. Review exceptions before choosing a record to keep.

An audit can flag candidates while leaving the source unchanged. Removal belongs in cleanup only when the keep/remove rule is unambiguous.

Common traps

  • Leading or trailing spaces can hide matches.
  • Uppercase and lowercase text may refer to the same value.
  • Blank identifiers can group unrelated rows together.
  • Two legitimate events can share a customer or product name.
