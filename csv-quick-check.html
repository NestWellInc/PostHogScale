    Privacy and security

    This page has no external code, analytics, forms, cookies, uploads, or browser storage. Selecting a file gives this page temporary, read-only access to its contents for this check. Results disappear when you clear them, close, or reload the page.

    For extra caution, do not select confidential data on a shared or untrusted device.

    What this check can—and cannot—tell you

    It reports parsed data-row and column counts, blank cells, exact duplicate data rows, and rows whose field count differs from the header. It supports standard quoted CSV fields, escaped quotes, commas, and line breaks inside quotes.

    This is a basic structural screen, not a complete audit. It does not validate business rules, required fields, types, identifiers, near-duplicates, delimiter variants, formulas, malicious spreadsheet content, or whether unusual values are correct. Files are decoded as UTF-8; invalid byte sequences are reported, but legacy encodings can still produce misleading characters. Counts depend on parsing and may differ from spreadsheet software.
