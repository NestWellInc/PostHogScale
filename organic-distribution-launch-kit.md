# ProjectScale Organic Distribution Launch Kit

Use this only after the destination's current rules have been checked and the owner's account legitimately qualifies. Do not automate posting, mass-message users, karma-farm, evade account-age/activity gates, or conceal commercial intent.

## Primary destination
Send qualified prospects to the public ProjectScale service chooser/catalog or, once published, the Fiverr Gig. Never post Stripe checkout links where a marketplace/community forbids off-platform sales.

## Offer positioning
- $15 Data Quality Audit: bounded diagnostic/review.
- $35 Cleanup + Normalization: cleanup and normalization for one supported file.
- $49 Contractor Job-Cost CSV Normalization: specialized contractor/job-cost structure.
- $99 Multi-File Processing Sprint: up to 10 supported CSV files under the published scope.
- $59/month Data Maintenance: recurring service for demonstrated repeat needs.
- Fiverr intended ladder: $15 Basic / $45 Standard / $95 Premium reusable workflow, subject to Fiverr's live required fields.

## Rules-first preflight
Before every post:
1. Read the community's current rules on the day of posting.
2. Confirm `[For Hire]`/commercial/self-promotion is explicitly permitted.
3. Confirm the owner's account meets age/activity/karma requirements naturally.
4. Use the exact title/tag format required by that community.
5. Respect frequency limits.
6. Do not post if rules are ambiguous or prohibit AI-assisted/commercial work.
7. Do not claim credentials, experience, turnaround, accuracy, security, compliance, or platform expertise that cannot be substantiated.

## Reply qualification template
Before quoting, establish:
- file type(s) and approximate row/file count;
- desired output and required column/schema rules;
- duplicate/validation rules;
- whether this is one-time or recurring;
- whether any external system/API is involved and whether access/automation is authorized;
- deadline requested by buyer (do not promise until scope is reviewed).

Route one-time bounded work to the published service tier that fits. Route repeated stable-layout PDF/CSV or repeat transformations toward a reusable workflow or recurring maintenance only when the need is real.

## Distribution priority after Fiverr publication
1. Communities whose current rules explicitly permit `[For Hire]` or commercial service posts and whose owner account naturally qualifies.
2. Existing professional/social profiles where truthful self-promotion is permitted.
3. Fiverr external promotion where permitted, linking to the public Gig rather than attempting unsolicited Fiverr DMs.
4. Direct ProjectScale chooser/catalog where off-platform commercial links are permitted.

## Measurement
Track only meaningful funnel events that can be defensibly observed: published posts, qualified inquiries, quotes, completed paid transactions, actual fees, and verified net cash. Do not count impressions, paper gains, hypothetical contract values, or unverified checkouts as revenue.
