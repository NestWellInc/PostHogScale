Frequently asked questions

What file formats are supported?

These offers support UTF-8 or UTF-8-SIG CSV input. Other formats require separate scope.

Will my original file be changed?

No. The original source is preserved during processing.

What happens when a requirement is ambiguous?

Ambiguous requirements, exceptions, and worker failures are routed for review rather than silently producing a questionable result.

Can you connect directly to an external system?

External-system integrations are outside these listed offers and require separate scope.
