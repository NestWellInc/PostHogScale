## Buyer qualification

Before accepting work, establish:
1. What file format(s) are supplied?
2. Approximate rows/files/pages?
3. What exact output is required?
4. Which fields/formulas must be preserved?
5. Is the data authorized for the buyer to provide and for us to process?
6. Is this one-time work or likely recurring?

Route larger/repeat needs toward the ProjectScale higher-value ladder rather than expanding starter scope for free.
