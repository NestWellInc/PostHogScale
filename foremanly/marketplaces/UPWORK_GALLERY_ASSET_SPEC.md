## Current Upwork constraints verified 2026-08-19
Primary source: Upwork Help, “How to add images and video to your Project Catalog project.”

- JPEG or PNG images, under 10 MB.
- Use 4:3 aspect ratio; Upwork says images should look sharp at 1000 × 750 and be no larger than 4000 px on either dimension.
- Up to 20 images may be added, but Foremanly should launch with a small high-signal set rather than filling slots.
- Upload order matters because images cannot be reordered after they are added.
- Avoid text-heavy, clickbait, blurry, distorted, poorly cropped, watermarked, unrelated, or competitor/company-referencing visuals.
- Do not include phone numbers, email addresses, social accounts, or other contact information.
- Do not use logos other than Foremanly’s own authorized logo.
- Project Catalog requires a picture or video cover; a PDF work sample cannot serve as the cover.

Upwork also states that gallery quality/accuracy is part of Project Catalog review. Therefore every Foremanly gallery claim must be traceable to the synthetic source package below.
