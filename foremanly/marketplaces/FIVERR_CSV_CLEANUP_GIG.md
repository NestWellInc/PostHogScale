## Description
I clean and validate clearly defined CSV or Excel spreadsheet projects while preserving your original source file unchanged.

Typical checks include duplicate records under an agreed rule, missing or blank values, inconsistent dates, casing, whitespace, numeric and identifier formats, malformed rows, shifted fields, and inconsistent headers.

Standard cleanup orders include:
- cleaned CSV or spreadsheet working copy
- concise quality report
- change and exception documentation
- post-cleaning validation against the agreed checks

I do not silently invent missing data or guess ambiguous duplicate rules. Unclear cases are flagged for your decision.

Before work begins, please provide the source file, desired output format, cleanup goal, duplicate/removal rules, required fields, and any destination specification. Larger, multi-file, destination-mapping, sensitive-data, or reusable automation projects may require separate scope.

Synthetic portfolio samples are available and are clearly labeled as demo data.

## FAQ
### Will you overwrite my original file?
No. The operating rule is to preserve the original source and work from a copy.

### Do you remove duplicates automatically?
Only under an agreed rule. Ambiguous duplicates can be flagged instead of removed.

### Will you fill missing values?
Not by guesswork. Missing values stay blank/flagged unless you provide an explicit deterministic rule.

### What do I receive with cleanup?
The cleaned working file, a concise quality report, change/exception documentation, and validation against the agreed checks.

### Can you prepare a file for Shopify or another system?
Yes when the appropriate package/scope is used and you provide the current destination requirements. Platform acceptance is not guaranteed because account configuration and current rules can affect imports.

### Can you work with confidential or sensitive data?
Contact Foremanly before sending it. Suitability and handling must be confirmed rather than assumed.
