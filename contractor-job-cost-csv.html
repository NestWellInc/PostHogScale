Contractor Job-Cost CSV Normalization

Job-cost exports often use inconsistent labels or formats across jobs, vendors, and cost categories. Normalization can make one contractor CSV more consistent when the target rules are explicit.

Fields to review before normalization

  • Job or project identifiers
  • Cost categories and cost codes
  • Vendor or subcontractor names
  • Transaction dates
  • Amount and quantity fields

The exact columns vary by export. A source system integration or a non-CSV file requires separate scope.

Define the target conventions

Document the preferred label for known category variants, the required date representation, and whether identifiers may contain leading zeroes. Avoid assuming two similar vendor or job names are the same entity without a rule.
